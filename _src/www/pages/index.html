How Enrollment Works

Everything happens on this website except the payment, which our office confirms by hand.

  1. Create an Account

    Sign up with the student's and guardian's details. It is free.

  2. Choose a Course

    Pick the class, version and batch that suit your child.

  3. Pay Your Way

    bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank transfer or cash at the office.

  4. Office Confirms

    Our team checks the payment and enrolls your child.

  5. Student Portal Opens

    You receive a Student ID and PIN for the private Student Portal.
