What Is the Difference Between Bangla and English Version?

It is the medium your child studies in at school. Both batches follow the same syllabus and the same exams, in their own language.

How Do Online Classes Work?

Enrolled students sign in to the private Student Portal with their Student ID and PIN and tap Join Class. Only students of that batch can enter.

How Do We Pay?

Apply from your account, then pay by bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank transfer or at the office. Our team confirms the payment and enrolls your child.

Is There a Trial Class?

Yes, one live class is free for every new student. Book it here.
