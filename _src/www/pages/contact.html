Help and FAQ

Account, payment and access questions.

I Paid. When Will My Child Get Access?

Usually within one working day. The office checks the transaction ID and enrolls your child. The Student ID and PIN appear in your account and are sent by SMS.

I Forgot My Account Password.

Call the helpline from the mobile number on the account and the office will reset it.

Can Two Children Use One Account?

Each student needs their own account, because each one gets a personal Student ID. Guardians can use the same mobile number for both after talking to the office.
