About QEC Academy

শেখার ভিত্তি হোক এখানেই

Quarantine Education Care began as a way to keep children learning when schools closed. Today QEC Academy teaches Class 4 to 6 online and at the centre, with one goal: a foundation strong enough for Cadet College and everything after it.

How We Teach

Small batches by class, version and shift. Every lesson is followed by practice, every week ends with an exam, and every guardian sees the result.

Our teachers write the QEC Mastering books we teach from, so class, homework and tests all speak the same language.

What Every Student Gets

  • A Student ID and a private Student Portal
  • Live classes that only their batch can join
  • Weekly exams and monthly model tests
  • A Google Classroom for notes and homework
  • A helpline that answers on WhatsApp
