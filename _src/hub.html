Try the Enrollment Flow in Two Minutes

Everything except the payment itself is automatic. The sales team confirms the payment by hand.

  1. On the website, open a course and press Apply Now, then create an account.
  2. In My Account, send sample payment details (any 6+ letter transaction ID, for example TEST12345).
  3. Open the Sales Desk (70003 / 1234), find the application under Payment to Check and press Verify and Enroll.
  4. Back in My Account the Student ID and PIN appear. Press Open Student Portal and sign in.
