Private portals are hidden from search engines, never linked from the public menu, and only answer on their own subdomain. At launch the QEC server checks every sign-in and every request, so a portal shows nothing to anyone without the right role.

Enrollment Flow

  1. Sign up on qecacademy.com (student and guardian details, password).
  2. Apply for a course and batch from the course page.
  3. Pay manually: bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank or cash; the guardian enters the transaction ID.
  4. Sales verifies the payment on sales.qecacademy.com and presses Verify and Enroll.
  5. Access opens: Student ID and PIN are created, sent by SMS and email, and the Student Portal works.

Only step 3 is manual. Online payment (SSLCommerz, bKash API) can replace it later without changing the other steps.
