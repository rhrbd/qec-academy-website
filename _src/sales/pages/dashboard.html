How Approval Works

  1. The student signs up on qecacademy.com and applies for a course.
  2. The guardian pays by bKash, Nagad, Rocket, bank or cash, and sends the transaction ID.
  3. You check the transaction in the merchant or bank statement, choose the batch and press Verify and Enroll.
  4. The system creates the Student ID and PIN and opens the Student Portal for that student.
