Questions guardians ask

If yours is not here, call 01841-233235.

What is the difference between Bangla and English version?

It is the medium your child studies in at school. Both batches follow the same syllabus and the same exams, in their own language.

How do online classes work?

Your child signs in to the student dashboard with a QEC ID and PIN and taps Join class. Only students of that batch can enter the room.

Can we switch from online to offline later?

Yes. Tell the office and your child moves to the centre batch of the same class and version from the next week.

Is there a trial class?

Yes, one live class is free for every new student. Book it here.
