QEC মেধা অন্বেষণ ২০২৬

QEC's scholarship exam for Class 4, 5 and 6. Students sit it online or at the centre, in Bangla or English version, and top scorers win a scholarship for the new session.

Check registration Student login

How it works

The live exam site is medha.qecacademy.com. This page will link to it every year.

  1. Register

    Fill the form. You receive an Exam Roll and a PIN by SMS.

  2. Check your status

    Use your mobile, name and roll to confirm you are eligible.

  3. Open the dashboard

    Log in with your roll and PIN to see the exam time and link.

  4. Sit the exam

    Online on the exam day, or at the centre on paper.

  5. See your result

    Results and scholarship news appear on the dashboard.
